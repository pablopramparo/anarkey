# Brand assets

The code of Anarkey is MIT-licensed (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)). **The name "Anarkey" and its logo are not.**

## What this covers

- The name **Anarkey** and the slogan **Free the music**.
- The logo, the isotype and the app icon, wherever they appear:
  - the PNG files in this folder (`anarkey_ico`, `anarkey_iso`, `anarkey_logo`, `anarkey_logo_slogan`);
  - their copies in the app (`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/brand_*.png` and the launcher and splash drawables
    that use them);
  - the store graphics in `store/graphics/`.

## What you can do

- Build the app from source, run it and study it.
- Fork the project, modify it and redistribute it under the MIT license.
- Say that your work is based on Anarkey, link to this repository and describe it accurately.
- Show screenshots of Anarkey when you write about it.

## What you cannot do

- Publish a modified build, a fork or a different app under the name Anarkey, or with its logo or icon, in a way
  that could make people think it is the original project.
- Use the logo or the name to suggest that the maintainers endorse your work.

If you distribute a fork, change the app name, the application id (`org.anarkey.app`), the icon and the logo. The
code under MIT is yours to reuse; the identity is what tells people which app is the original.

## Origin of the artwork

The isotype was generated with an AI image tool and then edited by the maintainer. Depending on the country, artwork
generated with AI may have limited copyright protection. This page states how the maintainers want the brand to be
used, whatever its legal status.

The assets are declared as AI-created in the Google Play listing for the same reason.
