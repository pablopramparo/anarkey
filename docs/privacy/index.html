Anarkey privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short: Anarkey does not collect, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not use the Internet permission, so it has no way to send anything off your device.

Microphone

The tuner analyses sound from the microphone in real time to find the pitch. That audio is processed on your device and is neither saved nor sent anywhere. The recorder saves audio on your device only when you start a recording.

What is stored on your device

  • Your recordings, recording sessions and markers.
  • Your songs, notes, chords and favourite chord shapes.
  • Your preferences: language, reference pitch, note names and metronome settings.

All of this stays in the app's private storage. The app turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for it, so it is not copied to your Google account or to a new phone. You can delete recordings and songs inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

What you choose to share

If you use Share or Export on a recording, Android hands the file to the app or folder you pick. Anarkey does not control what that app or service does with it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: for the tuner and the recorder.
  • Foreground service (microphone and media playback): so a recording or the metronome keeps working when the screen is off, shown with a visible notification.
  • Wake lock: so the metronome keeps its timing while the screen is off.
  • Notifications: to show that notification.

The app does not ask for Internet, location, contacts, camera, storage or any other permission.

Third parties

Anarkey includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries. It is free and open-source software built with open-source libraries that do not send data. The source code is public on GitHub, so any of this can be checked.

Children

Anarkey is not directed at children, and because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Developer builds

Test builds for developers can save a diagnostic audio file on the device when the developer asks for it. This feature is not present in the version distributed to the public.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact
