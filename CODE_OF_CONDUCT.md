# Code of conduct

Anarkey is a small, friendly project made by people who like music and free software. We want
contributing to it to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their level, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with beginners, with people who write in a second language and with slow replies.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and take feedback the same way.
- Keep discussions about the project and the music.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome in a project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may
ban people who do so repeatedly or seriously. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content**
option on the comment or issue. For anything else, open an issue titled "Conduct report" without
details and a maintainer will arrange a private channel. Reports are treated privately.

This applies to the repository, its issues and discussions, and any space where someone represents the
project.
